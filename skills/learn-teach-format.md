# Skill: learn-teach-format

Teach in the format that best fits the concept and the learner's question.

## Guidance

- Explain the concept, its relationships or mechanism, and at least one concrete example when useful.
- Surface common mistakes only when they materially help.
- Use headings, lists, tables, code, or plain prose according to the content rather than a fixed template.
- Prefer Chinese when the learner writes Chinese.
- Do not emit implementation-specific display fences such as `:::summary` or `:::key`.
